# `desc` compatibility

Generated by `task desc-compat`. A **soft, one-directional gauge, never a
gate**: it measures the fixed point `desc_normalize(arity(x)) == arity(x)`
after normalizing away the deviations arity keeps on purpose. A divergence
caused by arity *preserving a comment* is not a divergence to fix.

| Width | Cases compared | `desc` leaves unchanged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 75 (`desc`'s own `strwrap` width) | 24 | 18 (75.0%) |
| 80 (arity's default) | 24 | 17 (70.8%) |

## Recorded intentional deviations

- **Comments are preserved.** `desc` drops every one, even on a plain
  parse-and-write. Normalized away above; never a triage item.
- **No trailing space after a field with an empty own line.** `desc`
  writes `Depends: `; we do not emit trailing whitespace.
- **`R` sorts first in `Depends`.** `desc` sorts it alphabetically among
  the packages, which reads as a mistake to anyone who writes R.
- **`Authors@R` is formatted as R**, by arity's own formatter rather than
  round-tripped through `deparse()`. This is the whole point of the feature.

## Divergences

Read each against the recorded deviations above before treating it as a
question. What is left over is the signal: a place where arity and `desc`
disagree about a rule neither of us chose to differ on.
