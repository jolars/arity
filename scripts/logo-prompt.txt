Flat vector mascot logo for a developer tool. The image is the text "f(x, y,
z)" written horizontally in one row, in a lowercase font, where
each letter is a living character but still clearly readable as its
letter. The parentheses "(" and ")" and the two commas are PLAIN
punctuation, NOT characters.

Each letter keeps its real letter shape and gains two simple dot eyes, eyebrows, a mouth
and stick arms and legs with little hands and feet. Read
left to right:

- A purple lowercase letter "f" as the host character, standing just before the
  opening parenthesis oriented towards the viewer but with glancing sideways at
  the rest of the characters, with a slightly exasperated expression of raised
  eyebrows and nervous smile. Arms akimbo, with one hand on its hip and the
  other hand gesturing towards the rest of the characters as if introducing
  them.
- An opening parenthesis "(".
- A teal-green lowercase letter "x" as a distracted character, looking down
  and fingering the following comma with one hand, while the other hand is in its pocket.
- A comma.
- A yellow lowercase letter "y" that's pointing angrily at the "z" with one arm
  and scowling with furrowed eyebrows and a downturned mouth, while looking
  towards the "f".
- A comma.
- A blue lowercase letter "z" that's fallen asleep, with closed eyes
  and three little "Z" shapes floating up above it to indicate snoring.
  Slumped over.

The letters must stay legible so the whole image still reads as the text "f(x,
y, z)". Plain solid white background, generous margin, centered composition,
crisp clean edges suitable for vector tracing. The parenthesis should be
taller than the letters.
